THE STORY

From a street in Ireland to a factory floor in San Diego.

It started with design. A product design degree in Dublin, and a decade of making things with my hands after it.

In New York I worked hospitality to pay for it while I freelanced installations — lighting for bars and restaurants, built and fitted myself. That turned into installing high-end kitchens, floor to ceiling, where a millimetre out is a job done twice.

Then I built a home inside a cargo trailer, lived in it for six months, and drove it across the country. I stopped in San Diego.

I became Head of Manufacturing at a utility manufacturing company. For the last three years I've personally designed the product and owned everything after it — production, ordering the parts, and getting it deployed in the field.

For the last year I've been building with AI. Not demos — solutions to the things that actually go wrong on a factory floor. I built what the company and I needed, and then I packaged every one of them up for anyone who needs the same. Explore my builds below.

THE FOUNDATION · EVERYTHING BELOW STANDS ON IT

Every agent I run reads and writes the same vault. That is the whole trick. An agent with no memory is a chatbot — it re-learns your context every time and forgets the answer the moment it finishes. Give a fleet of agents one shared, permanent, searchable memory and they stop being demos: they hand work to each other, they remember what was decided six months ago, and they get better the longer they run. The platforms and the crew further down are what that made possible.
