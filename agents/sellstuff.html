Snap. Send. Sold.

That is the whole job. You take a photo of the thing you want gone and send it to his Telegram chat. Nothing posts until you tap Approve.

One photo in. A full listing out.

He reads the picture and writes everything a buyer needs: the title, the description, the condition, the category, an asking price and the lowest price he would take, with his reasons. When real sold prices exist, he prices from those.

Run the whole stall from your phone

Every item arrives as a card in Telegram. Reply to it and he acts: type 45 to set the price, 45/20 for price and floor, why to hear his reasoning, status to see what is live.

He posts. He never goes rogue.

Posting runs in a real Chrome browser on your own computer, logged into your own Facebook account. He posts, edits prices and takes listings down. He never opens buyer messages.

Get your own

The full agent is free on GitHub: the Telegram brain and the Facebook poster. Setup takes about an hour if you have used Cloudflare before.

  1. Make a Telegram botTalk to BotFather, get a token. That chat becomes his inbox.
  2. Deploy his brain to CloudflareA free Worker with a small database and photo storage. Add your Anthropic API key.
  3. Install the poster on your computerLog Chrome into Facebook once. He drives that browser to post, edit and remove.
  4. Send him a photoTap Approve on the card he sends back. That is it.

Automating Facebook Marketplace may break Facebook's terms. Run it on your own account, at your own risk. The safety rails keep him slow and careful on purpose.
